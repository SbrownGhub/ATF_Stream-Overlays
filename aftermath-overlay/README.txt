Drop transparent headshot PNGs in this folder.

Filename only goes into the controller — e.g. pantoja.png

Slot sizes (before the 0.8 level scale):
  back layer  148 x 176   — the fighter on the card
  front layer 166 x 196   — the prospective next opponent

Images are fit with object-fit: contain, anchored bottom-centre.
Head-and-shoulders crops work best, chin roughly a third down.

The right column mirrors images horizontally so fighters face inward.
Logos and sponsor patches will mirror too — flip those files first.
